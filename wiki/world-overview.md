# World Overview

NG is set in an original dark low-fantasy borderland. The current playable slice is centered on **Wolfpine Road**, a dangerous route leading toward the starving village of Wolfpine.

## Tone

The world is grounded, harsh, and political rather than heroic.

Common themes:

- hunger and scarcity
- unreliable authority
- desperate village politics
- old faiths and broken shrines
- smugglers and bandits
- border guards with divided loyalties
- ruined imperial remnants
- choices with consequences

## The Borderlands

The first region is a frontier where distant lords claim authority but local survival matters more than law.

Roads are unsafe. Villages depend on supply caravans. Old shrines and abandoned stones hint at older powers that the current rulers do not fully understand.

## Wolfpine

Wolfpine is planned as the first village hub.

Current facts:

- Wolfpine depends on supply caravans.
- A caravan has vanished on the road.
- Captain Renna is involved in protecting or investigating the village.
- The surrounding road is threatened by bandits and possible smuggler activity.

Planned role:

- first quest hub
- first companion recruitment space
- first faction-pressure location
- first place where the player sees consequences from choices

## Wolfpine Road

Wolfpine Road is the current test area.

Important locations:

### Old Road Shrine

A cracked roadside shrine where the old sun emblem has been scratched away. Fresh wagon ruts vanish into the trees nearby.

Current gameplay role:

- inspection hotspot
- updates `missing_caravan` quest to `found_wreck`
- sets `wolfpine_old_shrine_inspected`

### North Road

A current exit stub pointing toward the future Wolfpine Village area.

## World identity in one sentence

A starving frontier province where old powers, desperate people, and broken authority collide around a small band of dangerous nobodies.
