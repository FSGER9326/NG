# Characters and Companions

This page tracks known characters, companions, enemies, and planned party identity.

## Captain Renna

**Current role:** NPC / quest giver  
**Faction:** Wolfpine Guard  
**First seen:** Wolfpine Road

Captain Renna is a hard-eyed border captain whose loyalty to Wolfpine is stronger than her loyalty to distant lords.

Current gameplay role:

- clickable NPC marker
- opens dialogue
- can start the `missing_caravan` quest

Known dialogue themes:

- Wolfpine is starving.
- A caravan vanished two nights ago.
- Renna suspects more than simple banditry but cannot say everything openly.

## Brannoc of the Black Pines

**Current role:** planned companion / data stub  
**Faction:** none  
**Archetype:** woodsman, frontier survivor

Brannoc is a quiet woodsman with a hunter's patience and a long memory for broken promises.

Current gameplay state:

- companion data exists
- dialogue data exists
- not yet recruitable in the playable prototype

Planned gameplay role:

- survival/ranged-focused companion
- local wilderness knowledge
- possible Old Faith sympathy
- useful in road, forest, and tracking encounters

## Border Bandit

**Current role:** enemy placeholder  
**Faction:** Road Bandits  
**First seen:** Wolfpine Road

A hungry road thief with a dull blade and worse judgment.

Current gameplay state:

- appears as clickable placeholder marker
- combat is not implemented yet

Planned role:

- first simple enemy type
- used to test combat, loot, and surrender/mercy choices later

## Party identity

The player is not intended to begin as a chosen hero. The starting fantasy is a small band of dangerous nobodies entering a desperate frontier.

Planned party features:

- companions with opinions
- backgrounds that affect dialogue
- faction consequences
- practical survival pressure
- tactical combat roles

## Future companion design goals

Companions should not only be stat blocks. Each should eventually have:

- a role in combat
- a social/worldview angle
- personal quests
- interjections in major dialogues
- approval/disapproval triggers
- possible faction conflicts
